The one line a gamer texts the boss to skip work on GTA 6 launch week: "Sorry, stuck waiting in for the gas engineer, he gave me 8 till 6 and I've missed him twice already." Boring, specific and self-owning: a gas-engineer appointment window already missed twice. No wild story, no fever, no flat tyre, and no mention of the game — a real person texting in a hurry would never confess the launch in the excuse.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the excuse to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt` by default), verifying first that it is one line of at most 20 words and 1–140 characters with no dash.
